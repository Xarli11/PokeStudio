# PokeStudio.app — Brand Brief v1

## Current name

**PokeStudio.app**

Treat this as the working official product name unless the owner changes it.

## Positioning

PokeStudio should feel like a premium Pokémon workspace/laboratory rather than a fan wiki.

It combines:

- discovery,
- analysis,
- creation,
- experimentation,
- competition,
- learning.

## Brand personality

- elegant,
- futuristic,
- simple,
- technical,
- premium,
- approachable to casual players,
- credible to competitive players.

## Product language

Primary pillars:

- **Explore**
- **Build**
- **Battle Lab**

Horizontal intelligence layer:

- **PokeStudio AI**

Potential feature naming:

- Damage Lab
- Matchup Lab
- Simulation Lab
- Replay Analyzer
- Battle Trainer
- AI Coach

Avoid naming every feature “Lab”; use it where experimentation/analysis genuinely fits.

## Visual constraints

- dark-first with a complete light theme,
- original PokeStudio palette,
- type colors are semantic accents,
- subtle motion,
- minimal information hierarchy with progressive disclosure,
- no default Poké Ball logo,
- do not imitate official Pokémon typography/logo treatment.

## Naming/legal status

The name has prior similar uses in the broader Pokémon ecosystem. Before significant public marketing spend, trademark filing or commercialization, perform dedicated clearance/legal review.
