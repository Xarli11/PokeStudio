# PokeLab — Monetization Principles

## Goal

Monetize sustainably without degrading the core product or community trust.

## Free core direction

Keep broad access to:

- Pokédex/reference data,
- Team Builder basics,
- damage calculation,
- core battle simulation,
- useful competitive data,
- collection basics,
- sharing basics.

Exact limits may evolve.

## PokeLab+ candidates

Prefer premium features with real cost or significant added value:

- advanced contextual AI,
- AI Coach,
- intensive replay analysis,
- large simulation batches,
- extended AI usage,
- deeper history/analytics,
- advanced personalization.

## AI economics

Never create a paid AI feature whose marginal provider cost makes pricing structurally unsustainable.

Track feature-level cost before setting quotas/prices.

## Other channels

Potentially:

- donations/supporter tier,
- subtle affiliate partnerships,
- selective sponsorships,
- API/developer plan later.

## Advertising

Avoid intrusive ads.

Never interrupt an active battle with interstitial/video advertising.

## Community principle

Monetization should feel like supporting/enhancing PokeLab, not paying to unlock basic Pokémon knowledge.
