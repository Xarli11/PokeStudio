# PokeLab — Licensing & IP Strategy (Planning Document)

**Not legal advice. Final public licensing and trademark/IP posture should be reviewed before launch.**

## First-party code intent

The owner wants:

- public/source-visible code,
- community contributions,
- noncommercial self-hosting permitted,
- modifications/forks for noncommercial purposes permitted,
- third parties prevented from commercially monetizing PokeLab without permission,
- the official PokeLab project able to monetize the service/product.

Because commercial-use restrictions are intended, describe PokeLab as **source-available**, not OSI Open Source.

## Candidate license direction

A standardized source-available license such as **PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0** is a candidate because it permits noncommercial use, modification and distribution.

Do not paste a modified homemade license into `LICENSE` without legal review.

Alternative PolyForm variants such as Perimeter/Shield may be evaluated if the intended restriction is specifically competitive offerings rather than all commercial use.

## Contributor rights

Before opening broad external contributions, choose a contribution model that preserves the owner's ability to operate and commercially license the official product.

Options to evaluate with counsel:

- Contributor License Agreement (CLA),
- copyright assignment model,
- inbound=outbound only if compatible with commercial goals.

Default project intent: use a CLA or equivalent clear contribution grant before accepting substantial external contributions.

## Third-party software

Maintain a dependency/license inventory.

Known intended candidates:

- Pokémon Showdown server/simulator — MIT,
- `@smogon/calc` — MIT.

Preserve upstream notices and verify any copied/vendored files separately.

Do not assume an upstream server license applies to a separate client repository or all related assets.

## Pokémon IP/assets

Pokémon names, characters, artwork, trademarks and related media are separate from software license questions.

Guidelines:

- PokeLab presents itself as unofficial/fan-made,
- do not imply endorsement by Nintendo, Game Freak or The Pokémon Company,
- maintain provenance for images/sprites/assets,
- do not assume official art is commercially reusable,
- make assets replaceable,
- avoid official-logo imitation,
- review monetization/IP posture before public commercial launch.

Suggested factual disclaimer direction:

> PokeLab is an unofficial fan-made project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nintendo, Game Freak, or The Pokémon Company.

Final wording should be reviewed in context.

## Trademark/name status

Current working product name: **PokeLab**.

There are prior uses of similar names in the Pokémon ecosystem. Before significant public launch/brand spend, perform dedicated trademark clearance and obtain legal advice if appropriate.
