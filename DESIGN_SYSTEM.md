# PokeStudio — Design System Direction

## Brand personality

PokeStudio should feel:

- elegant,
- futuristic,
- premium,
- minimal,
- confident,
- fast,
- technical without becoming developer-only.

Avoid looking like a generic fan wiki or a clone of official Pokémon interfaces.

## Themes

- Dark is primary.
- Light is fully supported.
- Respect system preference where appropriate.
- Persist explicit user preference.

## Color strategy

PokeStudio needs its own neutral/brand palette.

Pokémon type colors are **semantic accents**, not the brand palette.

Examples:

- Fire UI badge can use Fire semantic color.
- Water matchup cell can use Water semantic color.
- Navigation and primary CTAs should still look like PokeStudio rather than a rainbow of types.

## Layout

Mobile-first.

Desktop may expose denser analytical views, but the same workflow must remain usable on mobile.

Prefer progressive disclosure:

- show the important answer first,
- make detail inspectable,
- avoid permanent visual clutter.

## Motion

Subtle and functional.

Use motion for:

- state transitions,
- result changes,
- hierarchy,
- battle feedback,
- focus/selection.

Avoid decorative animation that delays interaction.

## Product navigation

Primary conceptual navigation:

- Explore
- Build
- Battle Lab

AI actions appear contextually across these sections.

## Battle Lab identity

Battle Lab may have a slightly more experimental/analytical presentation while remaining part of the same design system.

Potential modules:

- Battle
- Damage Lab
- Matchup Lab
- Simulation Lab
- Replay Analyzer
- Battle Trainer

## Typography

Choose modern, highly legible web fonts/system font strategy during branding/scaffold work.

Do not introduce licensed fonts with unclear redistribution/web rights.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG-friendly interaction and contrast from the beginning.

Requirements include:

- keyboard access,
- visible focus,
- semantic HTML,
- no color-only meaning,
- reduced-motion support,
- readable compact data tables on mobile,
- accessible battle controls.

## Branding status

`PokeStudio.app` is the current product name.

Do not create a logo derived from a Poké Ball or official Pokémon logo treatment by default.

The logo should be abstract, simple and animatable if possible.
